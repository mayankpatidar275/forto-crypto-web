## 1. Buy 100 FORTO Tokens

- Purchase FORTO on exchanges or via our presale — only **100 million FORTO** will ever exist.

---

## 2. Mint Your “Lifetime Lottery Ticket” NFT (Cost: 100 FORTO)

- Spend **100 FORTO** to mint a unique ERC-721 ticket on-chain.
- Ticket fee breakdown:
  - **60 FORTO** → Prize Pool
  - **35 FORTO** → Operations & Marketing
  - **4 FORTO** → Community & Ecosystem Rewards
  - **1 FORTO** → Burn (permanently removed)

---

## 3. Prize Pool Funding & Deflation

- **60 FORTO** immediately tops up the on-chain prize pool that powers the draws.
- **1 FORTO** is burned, steadily reducing the circulating supply.

---

## 4. Automatic Entry into Three Draws

- Your NFT ticket is automatically entered into **three equal monthly draws**:
  - Month 1 (M1)
  - Month 2 (M2)
  - Month 3 (M3)
- _Future upgrades may expand eligibility up to seven draws over a five-year span._

---

## 5. Three-Tier Prize Distribution

Each draw’s total pool (60 FORTO × number of tickets sold ÷ 3) is split into:

| Tier     | % of Draw Pool | Winners per Draw            | Prize Mechanics                                |
| -------- | -------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 🏆 Mega  | 50%            | 1 winner                    | 0.50 × Pool to a single ticket                 |
| 🥈 Mid   | 25%            | Multiple(~8.4% of tickets)  | 25% ÷ Mid winners, each prize = 4× Micro price |
| 🎟️ Micro | 25%            | Multiple(~33.6% of tickets) | 25% ÷ Micro winners                            |

- **Up to 42%** of participants win something in Mid + Micro combined.
- Mid winners ≈ 1/5 of the 42%
- Micro winners ≈ 4/5 of the 42%

- If there are **fewer than 3 tickets**, the smart contract still pays out **100%** of the pool by consolidating tiers.  
  _Example: The single ticket wins Mega + Mid + Micro._

---

## 6. Edge Cases & Guarantees

- If only 1 ticket in a draw, it wins **Mega + Mid + Micro = 100%** of pool.
- If 2 tickets:
  - One ticket wins **Mega (50%)**
  - Other wins **Mid + Micro (50%)**
- For large **NNN**, **W<sub>max</sub>** grows, splitting 42 % of participants into Mid/Micro tiers with smaller per-winner amounts.

---

## 7. Claim or Trade Your NFT

- All prizes (in FORTO) automatically pay out to the wallet holding the winning NFT.
- NFTs remain tradable on secondary markets.
- New owners inherit:
  - Any remaining draw entries
  - Any unclaimed prizes
