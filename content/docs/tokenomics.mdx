## 4. Proprietary Prize Distribution & Draw Schedule

### 4.1. FORTO FairDraw Protocol™ (Three Tiers)

Every draw’s total prize pool (collected from NFT ticket sales) is divided into three tiers:

#### Mega Tier (50% of Draw Pool)

- Exactly **1 winner** per draw.
- **Mega Prize** = 0.50 × (draw’s pool)

#### Mid Tier (25% of Draw Pool)

- Multiple winners, chosen after the Mega winner.
- **Total Mid-pool** = 0.25 × (draw’s pool)
- Each Mid winner’s prize is engineered to be **4×** each Micro winner’s amount.

#### Micro Tier (25% of Draw Pool)

- Multiple winners, chosen last.
- **Total Micro-pool** = 0.25 × (draw’s pool)
- Distributes smaller prizes to a broad base.

> Key Rule:
> After the single Mega winner is selected, up to 42 % of all participants (rounded down) win in Mid + Micro combined. Within that 42 %, the on-chain algorithm assigns 1/5 of “secondary winners” to Mid and 4/5 to Micro—ensuring each Mid prize = 4 × each Micro prize.

---

### 4.2 Lottery Eligibility & Schedule

Each ticket is valid for **3 consecutive draws**:  
**Month 1 (M1), Month 2 (M2), Month 3 (M3)**  
Future: Up to 7 draws per ticket over 5 years.

> 60% of ticket revenue goes to prize pool = 3 × 20% per draw.

---

### 4.3 Dynamic “Winner-Count” Algorithm

Let `N` = number of tickets.  
Let `Pool` = FORTO in one draw.

- Step 1: 1 Mega Winner → 50% of Pool
- Step 2: Compute max secondary winners (up to 42% of `N`)
- Step 3: Split into Mid (⅕) and Micro (⅘)
- Step 4: Calculate prize per winner
- Step 5: Handle low participation
- Step 6: Handle high participation
